## About NovaCart
NovaCart is a modern e-commerce store that offers a wide range of products including electronics, home essentials, fashion accessories, beauty products, and lifestyle items. Our mission is to provide high-quality products, affordable prices, secure payments, and excellent customer service.

---

## Shipping Policy
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days  
- Standard shipping takes 3–7 business days  
- Express shipping takes 1–3 business days  
- International shipping is available for selected countries  
- Tracking number is provided after shipment  

---

## Order Tracking
Customers can track orders using the tracking number sent via email.

If tracking is not available after 48 hours, contact support.

---

## Return Policy
- Returns allowed within 30 days  
- Items must be unused and in original packaging  
- Order number required for return  
- Customer pays return shipping unless error from NovaCart  

---

## Refund Policy
- Refunds processed after inspection  
- Takes 5–10 business days  
- Shipping fee is non-refundable (unless store fault)

---

## Order Cancellation
- Orders can be canceled before shipping  
- After shipping, cancellation is not possible  
- Return process applies after delivery  

---

## Damaged or Incorrect Items
- Report within 7 days  
- Provide photos  
- Replacement or refund will be issued after review  

---
